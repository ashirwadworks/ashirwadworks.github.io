Turnkey Projects

Glass, aluminium, furniture, flooring, painting, electrical and AC as one project.

Enquire about this service
Introduction

When several trades share one site, most delays come from the gaps between them.

On a turnkey project we take responsibility for the full scope: one plan, one schedule and one point of contact, from the bare shell to the finished space. You can also hire us for any single service.

  • Painting

  • Complete commercial interior execution

Applications

What you get
